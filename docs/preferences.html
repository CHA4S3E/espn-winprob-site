Display Settings

These are saved on this device and apply next time you load the main page.
Upset Watch history
In ESPN view, hovering over a past point on the chart can show whether Upset Watch was active at that moment in the game.
Confidence band
A shaded band around the win-probability line on ESPN and Timeline charts (not Postcard), showing roughly how much uncertainty the model still has -- wider early, narrower as the game becomes more decided.
Legacy views
Timeline and Postcard are being phased out in favor of ESPN and Forecast, and are hidden from the view switcher by default. Turn this on to bring them back.
Win-probability change popups
When a poll moves a team's win probability, a small colored "+X.X%" badge pops up next to the number about half a second later, then fades out after a few seconds -- similar to how election-night vote-margin changes are called out. Shows up on ESPN and Forecasting views.
Reduce animations
Turns off the pulsing glow on the Live badge, Upset Watch, and the kickoff countdown once it's within an hour of starting. Each alert stays visible as a plain static border/color -- only the pulsing itself is removed. Separate from your OS's own reduced-motion setting, which is always respected regardless of this toggle.

Debug

These bypass the normal condition each feature waits for, purely so the look can be previewed on demand -- not meant to be left on once you're done looking.
Year in Review
Once week 17 (the last week of the season) is complete, a "Year in Review" button appears at the top of the main page automatically. This lets you see it early, before the season has actually finished.
Championship Week
In week 17, before kickoff, a hero banner with a sweeping spotlight effect appears above the matchups, and cards get reordered/ribboned by playoff stakes (championship game first, then each placement game down the bracket). This forces that treatment on regardless of which week is actually selected.
Thanksgiving Week
Whichever week actually contains Thanksgiving gets falling leaves in the background, pumpkins and a leaf garland on ESPN/Forecasting cards, and a hint in the kickoff countdown in the days before. This forces that look on for whichever week is currently selected, regardless of the real schedule.
Christmas Week
Whichever week actually contains Christmas gets falling snow in the background and a string-light garland on ESPN/Forecasting cards, colored by each matchup's two teams -- if Upset Watch fires on a card, its lights switch to the alert color and pulse together. This forces that look on for whichever week is currently selected, regardless of the real schedule.
